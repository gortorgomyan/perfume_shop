The Story of Aura

Inspired by twilight over golden fields, Aura captures the warmth of fading sunlight and the calm that follows. Top notes of bergamot and saffron open into a heart of iris and hay absolute.

The base lingers with amber, musk, and a whisper of vanilla — intimate, radiant, and unmistakably refined.
